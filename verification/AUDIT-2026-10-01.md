## Needs Andrew's decision (not changed)

Ranked by how much a visitor would notice.

1. **/events is an empty page in the main nav.** Both of its sections are
   empty states ("No upcoming events", "Coverage archive coming soon").
   Suggest hiding it from the nav until there is one event.
2. **The Build checkboxes are visitor-controlled.** Anyone can tick Andrew's
   milestones; the tick is saved in their browser and drives the "0 of 4
   completed" meter, which sits next to three IN PROGRESS tags. The tap
   targets are also 19px on phones. Suggest read-only status from the data.
3. **Two goal pages.** /goals and /build ("Build in Public & Goals") cover
   the same ground with different numbers and styles.
4. **/cafe reel caption does not match its image.** "Ascaso Espresso
   Extraction & Latte Art Workflow" over a red-lit cocktail glass, labelled
   "Reel preview" with no reel. Needs the real still or a new caption.
5. **"Three brands, one builder"** (/brands/hey-this-is-andrew) lists two
   brands; Sip the Magic is missing.
6. **Press kit audience numbers** all show a dash placeholder (waiting on the stats).
7. **The homepage shows photos twice**: the elevator after About and the
   six-photo strip in Selected Work.
8. **Section numbers are fixed per component**, so pages that reuse them
   count oddly: About shows only "04"; the BE page goes 01 → 05.
9. **Goals lede reads "Goals / Building in public."** on the homepage, which
   looks like a placeholder.
10. **Cafe buttons all read "STOREFRONT LINK"**: six identical labels for six
    different products (unclear for screen readers and search engines).
11. **Favicon** is still drawn from the recreated wordmark design (an "H_"
    monogram), not Andrew's logo.
