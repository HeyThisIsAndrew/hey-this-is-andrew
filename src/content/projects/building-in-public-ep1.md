## Process

One camera, one lens, available light. The point of the pilot was speed:
prove the full pipeline (shoot to upload) could happen inside a weekend,
then tighten from there.

## What I learned

Perfectionism is the bottleneck. Shipping the pilot with rough edges taught
more than another month of planning would have.
