## Process

No C-stands on this job, so everything was handheld or balanced on the bar.
Key light from the speedlight bounced off the back bar, practicals doing the
rest. Shot wide open and let the highlights bloom.

## What I learned

In a real bar you light with what the room gives you. The shot list matters
less than reading the practicals in the first five minutes.
