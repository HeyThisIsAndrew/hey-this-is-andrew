## Decisions (all easy to revert)

- **/gear** now renders the collection-driven GearGrid. The old page
  hardcoded a second, diverged copy of the kit. Nav gear links point at
  GearGrid's anchors (`#gear-bodies`, `#gear-zooms`, ...).
- **/feed redirects to /latest.** They were two archives of the same
  content. The footer's "Feed" link was replaced by Work, Latest, Goals,
  Services, Now.
- **NEW tags last 7 days**, not HQ's 24 hours (this site posts weekly). A
  video whose real date the YouTube scrape cannot read is never tagged new
  (its date used to be the build time). No NEW tag on the accordion: its
  panels are brands, not dated content.
- Photos moved to `src/assets/instagram/` so Astro `<Image>` sizes them
  (responsive widths, intrinsic dimensions, lazy). The sync writes there.
- Added `/now`, `/rss.xml` (writing), a 404 page, a `noindex` option on
  BaseLayout, and a sitemap filter for the previews and the 404.
- The built-site audit now fails on dead anchors (an internal `#id` whose
  page lacks that id). It caught one: project rows linked `#projects` on
  pages that have none.

## Hero concepts (awaiting Andrew's pick)

Preview routes (noindex, not in the sitemap, not linked):
`/preview/hero-a/`, `/preview/hero-b/`, `/preview/hero-c/`. The current
carousel stays live with its bug fixes until a pick.

- **A: the brands are the hero.** Nav, then the accordion. Most HQ-like,
  shortest page, the brands are the first thing anyone sees. Costs: no
  personal identity above the fold except the nav wordmark; the latest
  video loses its hero slot (it lives in Selected Work).
- **B: statement hero.** Full-screen portrait, the stacked wordmark, one
  line, one CTA ("See the brands"). Strongest personal statement. Costs: a
  full screen before any brand or work; the most scrolling of the three.
- **C: identity band, then the brands.** A short band (wordmark, one line,
  the current "Now") and the accordion in the same first screen. Keeps who
  you are and what you run both above the fold. Costs: slightly busier top;
  the wordmark appears twice (nav and band).

**Recommendation: C**, with A as the close second. C gives HQ's "the
accordion is the hero" feel without losing the personal-site identity the
statement hero is reaching for, and it removes the carousel's rotation
(one less thing moving on the page).
